# GlowRide — WhatsApp Sales & COD Flow

WhatsApp is where the money is made. The ad gets them here; **this closes them.** Speed matters most — reply within minutes or the sale cools.

---

## THE GOLDEN RULE OF UAE COD

COD has a 10–20% refusal rate (customer not home, changed mind, fake order). **Always confirm before you dispatch.** A one-line confirmation message cuts refusals dramatically and saves you the courier fee on dead orders.

---

## FLOW

### Step 1 — Instant reply (set as WhatsApp quick reply / greeting)
> Hi! 👋 Thanks for your interest in the GlowRide RGB kit.
> It's **AED 149**, cash on delivery, delivered **tomorrow** anywhere in the UAE. 🔥
> Just send me your **name, area, and car model** and I'll lock in your order.

### Step 2 — Collect details
You need: name, full address/area, contact number (already have it), car model, quantity.
> Perfect ✅ Just to confirm so I can dispatch:
> 📍 [Area/Address]
> 🚗 [Car]
> 📦 [Qty] kit(s) — AED [total]
> 💵 Cash on delivery
> Shall I send it out today for delivery tomorrow?

### Step 3 — The confirmation lock (this is the COD safety net)
When they say yes:
> Done! ✅ Your GlowRide kit is dispatched. The driver will WhatsApp/call you before arriving tomorrow. Please keep **AED [total] cash** ready. 🙌
> I'll also send you a 2-minute install video once it's delivered. Enjoy the glow! 🌈

### Step 4 — After delivery (drives reviews + referrals)
> Hey [name]! Did the kit arrive okay? 😄 Here's the quick install video: [link]
> If you love it, a quick photo/video of your car glowing would mean the world — and I'll send you a discount code for your next order or for a friend. 🙏

## BUNDLE UPSELL (only after they've committed to 1)

When someone confirms 1 kit:
> By the way — most people grab a 2nd for the wife's/family car since delivery's already coming. 2 kits is **AED 269 instead of 298** (save AED 29). Want me to add one?

Low effort, occasionally doubles an order. Don't push hard — one soft mention.

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"Is it good quality / will it break?"**
> Totally fair. That's exactly why we do cash on delivery — you inspect it first, only pay if you're happy. Plus 7-day replacement on any defect. Zero risk to you. 👍

**"Will it fit my [car]?"**
> Yes 👍 It's universal — flexible adhesive strips that fit the dash and footwells of basically any car, including the [their car]. Powers from your USB/12V. Takes 10 mins.

**"Can you do a better price?"**
> The AED 149 is already the launch price (was 199). But if you grab 2, I can do AED 269 for both — that's the best deal I can offer. 🙌

**"Where are you based / is this legit?"**
> We're based here in the UAE and hold stock locally — that's how we deliver tomorrow. And you pay cash on delivery, so you're protected either way. 🙏

**"Let me think about it."**
> No problem at all! Just so you know, this is the launch batch at AED 149 — once it's gone the price goes back to 199. I'll keep one reserved for you for 24h if you want. 😊
