FAQ

Will this fit my car?

It is a universal 12V aftermarket kit designed for most cars. Fitment depends on cabin layout, trim gaps and power access.

Can I install it myself?

Yes, if you are comfortable with basic interior trim work. If you are the type to remove a panel with a kitchen knife, please involve someone competent before the car becomes modern art.

How long does delivery take?

For this validation batch, expected delivery is confirmed on WhatsApp before payment. Standard dropship delivery can take 7–15 business days depending on supplier dispatch and customs.

Is this OEM?

No. It is an aftermarket ambient lighting kit. OEM-style vehicle-specific kits may be offered later if the first batch proves demand.

What happens if the kit arrives defective?

We confirm supplier defect policy before accepting batch orders. Defective units are handled case by case with photo/video proof.
