# How to Upload Images - Super Easy Guide

### Best Way (Recommended):

1. Double-click on `upload-assets.bat` (if you are on Windows)
2. A black window opens
3. Type a number (1 to 6) → Press Enter
4. Copy your pictures into the folder it shows you
5. Copy and paste the 3 git commands it shows

Done! ✅

---

### Still Confused?

Just drag your image files into the `assets` folder on GitHub:

- Go to `assets` folder
- Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
- Choose the right subfolder (hero, generated, etc.)

**Never** put images outside the `assets` folder.
