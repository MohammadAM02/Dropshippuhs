## DAY-BY-DAY

### Day 1 (today)
- Buy/order 15 units locally. Pick the best one.
- Send 2 supplier RFQs (for restock).
- Set up WhatsApp Business number + greeting message (from the WhatsApp flow doc).
- Register GlowRide TikTok + Instagram accounts.

### Day 2
- **Film everything** — one night, the full shot list. ~30 min in a dark car park.
- Edit 3 ads (CapCut, 15s each, vertical). Don't overthink — raw wins.
- Deploy the landing page (open index.html locally to test, then push to Netlify/Vercel/Cloudflare Pages — drag-and-drop, free, 5 min). Update the WhatsApp number in index.html first.

### Day 3
- Launch ads. AED 50/day. Meta "Send Message → WhatsApp" + TikTok.
- Post all 3 clips organically too.
- Be glued to WhatsApp. Reply in minutes.

### Day 4–5
- Kill the worst ad. Keep the winner.
- First sales should land here. Confirm → dispatch → deliver.
- Post organic daily.

### Day 6–8
- Scale the winning ad to AED 70–80/day **only if** cost-per-message is healthy (<~AED 12).
- This is where we should cross break-even (~sale 7–8).
- Ask delivered customers for a photo/video → swap fake reviews on the page for real ones.

### Day 9–11
- Pure scaling. Push the winner. Refilm a fresh hook if the ad fatigues.
- Bundle upsell on every WhatsApp chat.

### Day 12–14
- Final push. Tally the scoreboard.
- Day 14: calculate profit. Positive = proven. Decide whether to scale into a real brand or pivot product.

## KILL SWITCHES (don't throw good money after bad)

- **AED 300 spent, zero WhatsApp messages** → creative is broken. Refilm the hook. Don't keep paying for a dead ad.
- **Lots of messages, no closes** → it's a price/trust problem or slow replies. Fix the WhatsApp flow, not the ad.
- **Sales but COD refusals high** → tighten the Day-3 confirmation step; only dispatch confirmed orders.
- **ROAS underwater by Day 10** → product or market is wrong, not the execution. Note it, kill it, move on. That's also a valid result — we'd have proven the *system* works even if this product doesn't.
