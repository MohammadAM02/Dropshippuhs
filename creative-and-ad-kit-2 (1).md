# GlowRide — Creative & Ad Kit (UAE, 14-Day Test)

The product is saturated globally. **Our creative is the only edge.** Raw, filmed-in-your-own-car footage beats polished studio ads here every time. Don't over-produce. Film on your phone, vertical, at night.

---

## THE ONE RULE

Every ad must show the **before → after reveal in the first 2 seconds.** Dark boring cabin → tap phone → cabin explodes with colour. That's the scroll-stopper. If the glow isn't on screen by second 2, the ad is dead.

---

## SHOT LIST (film all of this in ONE night, ~30 min)

Film in your own car, parked, dark surroundings (a quiet car park after sunset is perfect).

1. **The reveal** (most important): Phone in frame, cabin dark. Tap the app. Lights snap on. Slowly pan around the glowing interior. Film this 3–4 times in different colours (purple, red, ice-blue, green).
2. **Music sync**: Play a song, show the lights pulsing to the beat. 5 seconds.
3. **The install**: Hands tucking the strip along the dash/footwell. Quick, show how easy. 10 seconds.
4. **The drive-by POV**: Lights on, slow drive or just hands on wheel with glow around. Cinematic. 5 seconds.
5. **Unboxing**: Open the box, lay the parts out. 5 seconds.
6. **Color scroll**: Hold the phone, swipe through colours fast, cabin changing live. 5 seconds.

You now have raw material for 5+ different ads from one night of filming.

---

## 3 AD SCRIPTS (15 seconds each)

### Ad 1 — "The Reveal" (run this first, it's the strongest)
- **0–2s:** Dark cabin. Text on screen: *"My car at night… 😐"*
- **2–4s:** Tap phone. Lights explode purple. Text: *"…after 10 minutes 🔥"*
- **4–10s:** Slow pan around glowing interior, cycle 2–3 colours.
- **10–13s:** Quick music-sync clip.
- **13–15s:** Text: *"AED 149 · Cash on delivery · Delivered tomorrow in UAE"* + *"Order on WhatsApp 👆"*

### Ad 2 — "The Install" (targets the 'is it hard?' objection)
- **0–2s:** Hand holding the kit. Text: *"No tools. No mechanic. 10 minutes."*
- **2–9s:** Fast cuts of tucking strips in, plugging into USB.
- **9–12s:** Tap phone → full glow reveal.
- **12–15s:** Text: *"AED 149 · Pay cash when it arrives · UAE next-day"*

### Ad 3 — "POV Night Drive" (lifestyle/vibe, best for retargeting)
- **0–3s:** POV hands on wheel, cabin glowing, slow night drive. Trending audio.
- **3–8s:** Cut to colour changes from the phone.
- **8–12s:** Music sync to the beat.
- **12–15s:** Text: *"Turn every drive into a vibe · AED 149 · UAE"*

## CAMPAIGN SETUP

**Platforms:** TikTok + Meta (Instagram Reels priority). Start TikTok if budget is tight — cheaper reach in UAE for this product.

**Budget:** AED 50/day total to start. Split AED 30 TikTok / AED 20 Meta, OR go all-in on one platform if AED 50 is the cap.

**Objective:**
- If running WhatsApp click ads → optimise for *link clicks / messaging*.
- Meta: use "Send Message → WhatsApp" objective directly. This is the cleanest funnel — ad opens WhatsApp, skips the landing page entirely. Test this against landing-page traffic.

**Targeting (UAE):**
- Location: UAE, all emirates. (Tighten to Abu Dhabi/Dubai/Sharjah if delivery to far emirates is slow.)
- Age: 18–40
- Interests: Car modification, car accessories, automotive, Nissan Patrol, BMW, drifting, JDM, car detailing
- Gender: All (skews male but women buy as gifts)
- Language: English + Arabic

**Creative rotation:** Upload Ad 1, 2, 3 as separate ads in ONE ad set. Let the algorithm find the winner. Don't split into many ad sets on AED 50/day — you'll starve the data.

---

## DAILY OPTIMISATION RULES

- **Day 1–2:** Don't touch anything. Let it learn. Watch cost-per-WhatsApp-click.
- **Day 3:** Kill any ad with a click cost 2x worse than your best. Keep the winner.
- **Day 4+:** If one ad is clearly winning and you're getting WhatsApp messages, push budget to AED 70–80/day on the winner.
- **Benchmark to aim for:** cost per WhatsApp message under ~AED 8–12. If you're closing 1 in 4 conversations → that's ~AED 40 cost per sale on a 104 margin. Profitable.
- **Kill switch:** If after AED 300 spent you have zero WhatsApp messages, the creative is wrong — refilm the hook, don't keep paying.
