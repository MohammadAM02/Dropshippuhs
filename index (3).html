Do I really pay cash on delivery?

Yes. There's no payment on this page and no card needed. You order on WhatsApp, we confirm, and you pay the driver in cash when the kit is in your hands.

How fast is delivery, really?

We hold stock in the UAE, so it's next-day for Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah, and usually 1–2 days everywhere else in the country. We confirm the exact time with you on WhatsApp.

Will it fit my car?

It's a universal 12V/USB kit. The strips are flexible and adhesive-backed, so they fit along the dash, doors and footwells of practically any car — sedan, SUV or 4x4. Tell us your model on WhatsApp if you want us to double-check.

Can I install it myself?

Yes — about ten minutes, no tools. It powers from a USB port or 12V socket, so there's no wiring or fuse-box work. We'll send you a short install clip on WhatsApp.

What if it arrives faulty?

You can inspect it before you pay, so a dead unit never costs you anything. And if something fails within 7 days, send a photo or video on WhatsApp and we replace or refund it.

Who's actually behind GlowRide?

A small team based in Abu Dhabi. We're new, we hold our own stock locally, and every order is handled by a real person on WhatsApp — which is exactly why we let you pay only after you've seen the product.
